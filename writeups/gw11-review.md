# No Friends in Fantasy
## Gameweek 11 — Tarkowski scored -4 in 39 minutes, Frank's Man City stack drew 1-1, and Gakpo didn't turn up

Bruce 60, Ed 45, Tom 33, Andreas 32, Lewis 21, Frank 19. Liverpool beat Man United 4-0, Leeds won 4-1 at Chelsea, and Everton lost 4-0 at Brentford with a man sent off for it. The preview went two from three, which in a league this chaotic is a promotion. The week's real winner was the bench: 71 points were left sitting on them.

## VARcelona v BakersBoys
Andreas Elia 32 – 45 Edward Baker

The preview made a big thing of Newcastle v Arsenal, and it ended 2-2, which is the kind of result that insults everyone equally. Ed committed to the match and got 7 points from Gabriel, Konsa and Havertz, with Gabriel and Konsa on 1 each after conceding twice. Andreas hedged it and got 9 from Raya, Hall and Barnes. So the man who played both sides finished two points better than the man who picked one. That is not a strategy, that is a coin that landed on its edge.

Andreas started eight players who scored 2 points or fewer. Gibbs-White, Fernandes and Ndiaye got 2 each, Raya, Hall and Delap got 1 each, Gvardiol played 12 minutes for 1 and Kostoulas played 23 for 1. Only Silva (8), Barnes (7) and Araujo (6) did anything. The best score in his whole squad was Tzolakis, on 10 for a Hull clean sheet and seven saves, sitting on the bench, in the same match where Andreas had started Kostoulas against him. He owned the wall and the man who couldn't get past it, and he picked the man who couldn't. Andreas left 13 points on his bench. He lost by 13.

Ed's 45 was built on Szoboszlai (9, a goal, a clean sheet and a bonus) and the Spurs pair of Kinsky and Van Hecke (7 and 6). He left 23 points on his bench: Strand Larsen on 10, Becker on 7, Virgil on 6, and Saliba on his usual 0. Liverpool won 4-0 and Ed counted 15 from it and left another 13 sitting there, and he still won by 13. This is the least dignified way to win that exists, and it is still a win.

**The needle:** the benches. Ed left 23 points on his and won by 13. Andreas left 13 on his and lost by 13.

**Man of the match:** Szoboszlai, 9. A goal and a clean sheet against a United side that took four.

## Change Name v Teej FC
Frank Pilling 19 – 33 Tom Greenwood

Frank scored 19, which is a number you would be embarrassed to find in a bank account, never mind a fantasy team. Tarkowski played 39 minutes, conceded four goals in them, got sent off and scored -4. That is a goal every ten minutes and then an early bath, which is efficient if nothing else. In the same match Frank owned Kayode, Brentford's defender, who got 6 for the clean sheet, on his bench. He had the winning defender and the losing defender and started the one who got sent off. That is a ten-point swing from a single decision.

The Man City stack was a five-star wanted level in the preview. In real life it was Man City 1-1 Fulham and nobody came for anyone. Matheus N. played 0 minutes. Haaland got 2 in 90 minutes, Doku got 2, Donnarumma got 2 and Cherki played 20 minutes for 1. Four players, 7 points. I backed this stack in the preview and I'd like it noted that I regret everything. N.Jackson went from 11 last week to 2 at Villa Park. Rice got 3 and Timber got 1. Only Tavernier (8, an assist, a clean sheet and two bonus) did a day's work.

Tom won with 33, which is not a good score, and he left 19 points on his bench to do it. Roefs got 11 for a clean sheet, eight saves and three bonus. Verbruggen, who started, conceded two and got 2. That is a nine-point goalkeeper swing. Belloumi got 7 on the bench too. Gyökeres played one minute, got booked in it and scored 0, which takes his season total to a very tidy 11. Thiago, suspended until 5 December, stayed on the bench and scored nothing, exactly as advertised.

**The needle:** Brentford 4-0 Everton. Frank owned Kayode (6, benched) and Tarkowski (-4, started). A ten-point swing from one fixture, and he made the call.

**Man of the match:** Tavernier, 8, the only man in Frank's team who looks like he knew there was a gameweek on.

## Bruces Babies v Team Tippy

Bruce scored 60 and it was almost all Bournemouth. Hill got 14 with an assist, a clean sheet and three bonus. Truffert got 6 and Evanilson got 6. **The needle:** Bournemouth 2-0 Forest. Bruce's three Bournemouth players scored 26. Lewis's entire team scored 21.

**Man of the match:** Hill, 14, with Wirtz level on 14. A defender with an assist and three bonus, the kind of week Bruce earned by not touching anything.
