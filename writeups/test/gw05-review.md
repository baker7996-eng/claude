# No Friends in Fantasy
## Gameweek 5 — Bruce won by three with an unused goalkeeper worth eight on his bench

Man City put five past Sunderland and let three in doing it, which was somehow good business for two different managers in this league. Brighton took Arsenal apart 3-0, which was bad business for almost everyone else. Two of the three head-to-heads came down to single figures. The third wasn't close, and a Leeds team that didn't score had a lot to do with it.

## Teej FC v BakersBoys
Tom Greenwood 53 – 36 Edward Baker

Semenyo did the heavy lifting on his own: two goals and an assist in City's 5-3 win over Sunderland, plus two bonus, for 17 — one City midfielder outscoring nearly half of what Ed's entire XI managed between them (36). Tom didn't need much else, but he got it anyway: Leeds drew 0-0 at home to Palace, and Muharemović (6), Bogle (5) and Stach (5) turned a goalless afternoon into 16 points.

Ed's week was buried at the Amex before half of his team had kicked off. Brighton beat Arsenal 3-0 and Gabriel, Konsa and Havertz — three of his XI — returned four points combined. Palmer and Rogers went to Brentford with Chelsea and lost 3-0 as well, worth two apiece. Five starters, two matches, two sides that shipped three goals apiece, eight points total.

The one thing that actually worked was Liverpool's 1-0 at Bournemouth: Becker, Virgil and Isak (the goal, plus three bonus) for 24 between them. It just had nothing to pair with — Wissa played 90 minutes at Newcastle and missed a penalty for his trouble, Lewis-Potter's 3 sat unused on the bench, and Saliba logged zero minutes for the fourth gameweek running. Everyone in the group knows. Moving on.

**The needle:** one fixture paid Ed 24 points on its own; his other five fixtures paid him 12 between them.

**Man of the match:** Semenyo, 17 — two goals, an assist and two bonus in a 5-3 win.

## VARcelona v Team Tippy
Andreas Elia 53 – 49 Lewis Tipping

Andreas basically bought a ticket to St James' Park and let it pay him out. Hall scored in the 2-1 win over Hull for 13, Barnes set up both goals for 9, Thiaw added 4 and Fernandez-Pardo 2 — 28 points from four Newcastle players in one match. Kostoulas chipped in 10 from Brighton's rout, and when Khusanov was named to start and didn't kick a ball, Ndiaye came off the bench and returned 5 with an assist anyway.

Lewis had the better individual week and still lost. Brobbey scored a hat-trick at the Etihad — Sunderland lost 5-3 and he still took 17 for himself and 3 bonus. Ajer's 7 came from a clean sheet at Brentford, Kerkez's 6 and Gakpo and Barcola (3 each) came from Liverpool's win at Bournemouth, and Barry added 6 from Everton's narrow win over Ipswich. That's a proper 49.

It came down to who got unlucky in the right place. João Pedro started for Lewis and played zero minutes — no sub came on, the blank just stood. James also blanked, but Xhaka replaced him and found 2. Four points either way and it's a different scoreline; instead Lewis is still without a win in five.

**The needle:** 28 points from one Newcastle match against a striker who never left the tunnel.

**Man of the match:** Brobbey, 17 — a hat-trick for the team that still lost 5-3.

## Itch Isak v Bruces Babies
Frank Pilling 48 – 51 Bruce Agars-Smith

Groß had himself a night at the Amex: goal, assist, clean sheet, three bonus, 14 points — a blank at 1 in gameweek 3, then 17 in gameweek 4, now this. Two big weeks running. Frank matched him almost point for point with Tarkowski, who took an assist, a clean sheet and three bonus in Everton's 1-0 over Ipswich for 14 of his own. Two identical scores, cancelled out, which left the goalkeepers to actually settle it.

Trafford kept it tight at Leeds — a clean sheet, five saves, three bonus, 10 points from a 0-0. Donnarumma conceded three in a game City won by two anyway and got 2. Eight points between the two goalkeepers in a match Bruce won by three.

And Bruce didn't need the extra: Pickford kept a clean sheet at Everton, made four saves and took a bonus point for 8, and never left the bench. Frank, for his part, had Šeško start and play no minutes at all — De Cuyper came on in his place and returned 6 with a clean sheet at Brighton, which is the only reason it was close.

**The needle:** Bruce won by three with 8 unused points sat on his bench. Do with that what you will.

**Man of the match:** Groß, 14 — a goal, an assist, a clean sheet and three bonus.
