# No Friends in Fantasy
## Gameweek 5 — Groß and Tarkowski both go for 14, and neither of them made the top score of the week

Man City beat Sunderland 5-3, Brighton beat Arsenal 3-0, and across six squads that added up to a 17-point hat-trick on the losing side, a goalkeeper who outscored another goalkeeper by eight, and a Chelsea striker who started for Lewis and didn't play a minute. It's a normal week in this league.

## Teej FC v BakersBoys
Tom Greenwood 53 – 36 Edward Baker

Semenyo got two goals and an assist in City's 5-3 over Sunderland: 17 points, a third of Tom's total by himself. The rest of Tom's win was a Leeds side drawing 0-0 at home to Palace. Muharemović 6, Bogle 5, Stach 5. Sixteen points for a match nobody will remember, and a very Leeds way to win a head-to-head.

Ed's week died at the Amex. Gabriel, Konsa and Havertz all started, Brighton won 3-0, and the three of them managed four points between them. Palmer and Rogers then went to Brentford and lost 3-0 as well, which added two points each. Five of Ed's starters were playing for teams that let in three that weekend.

The annoying bit is that one fixture went perfectly for him. Becker 8, Virgil 8 and Isak 8 (the goal and three bonus) from Liverpool's 1-0 at Bournemouth: 24 points from three players. It just had to carry Wissa, who played 90 minutes of Newcastle's win for nil, while Lewis-Potter sat on the bench with 3. And Saliba made it five gameweeks, zero minutes. Ed knows. We move on.

**The needle:** Liverpool paid Ed 24. Arsenal and Chelsea paid him 8 for five players.

**Man of the match:** Semenyo, 17 — two goals, an assist and two bonus.

## VARcelona v Team Tippy
Andreas Elia 53 – 49 Lewis Tipping

Four points in it, and Lewis started João Pedro, who didn't play. He's Lewis's most reliable player: 11, 9, 1 and 12 in the first four weeks, 33 for the season. He picked the four-point defeat to sit it out, and his zero stood. Reece James also didn't play, and Xhaka came on for him and got 2 from the losing side of a 5-3. O'Reilly lasted five minutes.

Which is a shame, because Brobbey finally turned up. After 2, 2, 2 and 1 he scored a hat-trick against Man City for 17 points, and Sunderland still lost 5-3. Ajer 7 from Brentford's clean sheet, Kerkez 6 and Barry 6 (Everton's winner against Ipswich) mean Lewis's 49 was a proper score. He's still on zero wins.

Andreas won it from one game at St James'. Hall scored in the 2-1 over Hull for 13, Barnes set up both goals for 9, Thiaw added 4 and Fernandez-Pardo 2. That's 28 points from four Newcastle players in one match, which is less a head-to-head than a man backing a single horse and watching it win. Kostoulas added 10 from Brighton's demolition of Arsenal, and Ndiaye came in for Khusanov, who didn't play, and got 5 with an assist.

**The needle:** a four-point defeat and a striker on zero minutes. Lewis can do that sum himself.

**Man of the match:** Brobbey, 17 — a hat-trick at the Etihad, in a defeat. Someone buy him a drink.

## Itch Isak v Bruces Babies
Frank Pilling 48 – 51 Bruce Agars-Smith

Two 14-pointers cancelled each other out. Groß scored, assisted and kept a clean sheet as Brighton beat Arsenal, and took three bonus. It's his second big haul in a row, after 17 in gameweek 4. Tarkowski matched him for Frank with an assist, a clean sheet and three bonus in Everton's 1-0 over Ipswich. That left the goalkeepers to settle it.

Trafford kept a clean sheet at home to Palace, made five saves and took three bonus: 10 points. Donnarumma conceded three at home to Sunderland in a game City won by two, and got 2. That's an eight-point gap in a three-point match, before Enzo's goal in the same City game added 7 for Bruce.

Frank's four City players (Donnarumma, Matheus N., Cherki, Haaland) returned 17 between them from a 5-3 home win. Cherki and Haaland both scored, but City let in three, so there was no clean sheet for the back half. His Arsenal three of Timber, Rice and Tzolis managed 3 in total, and Timber was off after 59 minutes. De Cuyper came on for Šeško, who didn't play, and banked 6 with a clean sheet. Without that it wasn't close.

**The needle:** Trafford 10, Donnarumma 2, in a match decided by three.

**Man of the match:** Groß, 14 — a goal, an assist, a clean sheet and three bonus. That's back-to-back big weeks.
